Salescast

Forecast your sales from the files you already have

Upload a spreadsheet, CSV, JSON or PDF that has dates and sales figures. Salescast finds the right columns, studies trend, seasonality and outliers, tests ten forecasting models against your own history, and builds a report you can download.

What makes a good file
  • One row per sale, day or month, with a date column and an amount column. A product, region or channel column unlocks the breakdown.
  • Wide tables also work, such as one row per product with a column for each month.
  • At least 2 full years of monthly data, or a few months of daily data, lets the tool learn seasonality.
  • For PDFs, text-based reports work best. Scanned images can't be read.
